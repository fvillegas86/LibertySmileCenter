A bright, white smile is one of the first things people notice about you. Whether you have just completed a professional whitening treatment or simply want to keep your natural teeth looking their best, maintaining that brightness takes a combination of smart daily habits and regular dental visits. At Liberty Smile Center, we help patients across South Gate, Van Nuys, Whittier, La Habra, and Downey achieve and maintain radiant smiles.

## 1. Be Mindful of What You Eat and Drink

The foods and beverages you consume have the biggest impact on the color of your teeth. Certain items are well known for staining enamel over time.

**Common stain culprits:**
- Coffee and tea, which contain tannins that cling to enamel
- Red wine and dark-colored sodas
- Berries, tomato sauce, and soy sauce
- Dark fruit juices such as grape and cranberry

**Tips to minimize staining:**
- Drink staining beverages through a straw to bypass your front teeth
- Rinse your mouth with water right after consuming dark foods or drinks
- Wait about 30 minutes after eating before brushing, since acids can temporarily soften enamel

## 2. Brush and Floss Consistently

Daily oral hygiene is your best defense against plaque buildup, which can make teeth look dull or yellow.
- Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled brush and a whitening toothpaste
- Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth where your brush cannot reach
- An electric toothbrush can remove more plaque than manual brushing alone

## 3. Schedule Regular Professional Cleanings

Professional cleanings remove surface stains and tartar that brushing simply cannot. We recommend a cleaning every six months, or every three to four months if you are prone to staining. Cleanings also help prevent gum disease, which can affect the overall appearance of your smile.

## 4. Avoid Tobacco Products

Smoking and chewing tobacco are among the worst causes of tooth discoloration. Tobacco not only creates stubborn brown and yellow stains but also significantly increases your risk of gum disease and oral cancer. Quitting tobacco is one of the best decisions you can make for your smile and your overall health.

## 5. Ask About Touch-Up Treatments

If you have had professional whitening, ask your dentist about at-home touch-up kits. These usually include custom-fitted trays and a lower-concentration whitening gel that you can use periodically to maintain your brightness. Most patients benefit from a touch-up every six to twelve months.
